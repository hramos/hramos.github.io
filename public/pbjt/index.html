How to play

Tell the chef how to make a peanut butter & jelly sandwich. The chef does exactly what you say — no more, no less. If a step isn't in your instruction, it doesn't happen.

Getting started:

  • Everything starts sealed, bagged, and put away. Get things open and out first.
  • Things can be opened, taken out, picked up, and put on other things.
  • Spreading needs something to spread with… and something on the tool.
  • Slices have two sides. Think about which way the spread faces before stacking.

Good to know:

  • Be specific: "the left slice", "the peanut butter jar". Vague words get literal results.
  • The kitchen obeys physics — unsupported things fall, and the chef has exactly two hands and two arms of normal length.
  • The chef will tell you why when something is physically impossible.
  • The pantry shelf is full of things you never asked for. Words like "butter" are riskier than they look.
  • There are plenty of sillier actions to discover. Experiment.
